# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q: What services do you offer?**
**A:** We offer a range of services including web development, mobile development, site hosting, AI chatbots, automations & plugins, and APIs & integrations. See our services section for more details.

**Q: How much does a website cost?**
**A:** Our pricing varies depending on the project's scope and requirements. We offer free consultations to understand your needs and provide custom project pricing. We also have set pricing for some services, like our AI Chatbots starting at $60/month with a $500 setup fee.

**Q: Do you provide ongoing support and maintenance?**
**A:** Yes, we do. For example, our Site Hosting service includes managed hosting where we handle automatic backups, updates, security monitoring, and provide reliable support. We believe in building long-term partnerships with our clients.

**Q: How long does it take to build a website?**
**A:** The timeline depends on the complexity of the project. A simple brochure website might take a few weeks, while a complex web application could take several months. We will provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

**Q: What technologies do you use?**
**A:** We specialize in modern, high-quality technologies. Our preferred stack includes React and TypeScript for robust, scalable foundations. We also work with various other tools and platforms depending on the specific needs of the project.

**Q: Do you offer Search Engine Optimization (SEO) services?**
**A:** Yes, we build all our websites with SEO best practices in mind from the ground up. This includes fast loading times, responsive mobile-friendly designs, and semantic HTML structure to help your business rank higher in search results.

**Q: Will I own the code once the project is finished?**
**A:** Absolutely. Once the final payment is made, you own the intellectual property and the source code for the custom solutions we build for you. We provide full access and documentation so you have complete control over your project.

**Q: Are your applications scalable as my business grows?**
**A:** Yes! We design our web and mobile applications with scalable architecture in mind. Whether you're expecting a sudden spike in traffic or planning to add thousands of new users over the next year, our infrastructure solutions and backend systems are built to handle growth.

**Q: Can you integrate with third-party software we already use?**
**A:** Yes, custom APIs and integrations are one of our core services. We can connect your new application or website with your existing tools, such as CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot), payment gateways (Stripe, PayPal), or messaging services (Twilio, Slack).

**Q: How do you handle data security and user privacy?**
**A:** Security is a top priority. We implement industry-standard security protocols, including secure authentication, data encryption, and regular vulnerability patching. If you host with us, our managed hosting includes continuous security monitoring to keep your application and user data safe.
