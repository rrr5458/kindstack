# Company Overview

## Who We Are
We are a **Kansas City-based software development studio** with five years of experience building in fast-moving tech environments and startups. We specialize in finding real, practical ways to put emerging technologies and AI to work for real businesses.

## Our Philosophy
We believe clients deserve modern, powerful technology at a fair price. While tools that power great software have gotten more accessible, many agencies keep charging more. Our goal is to provide **modern stack** solutions and **dedicated support** that understand lean budgets and rapid iterations.

## Our Founder & Community Involvement
Before founding the studio, our founder spent **a decade in the classroom teaching high school students**. This shaped our core philosophy: we listen first, explain clearly, and never leave clients in the dark about what we're building or why.

We are deeply rooted in the Kansas City community and actively volunteer by:
- Teaching coding skills to the next generation
- Serving meals to neighbors experiencing homelessness
- Supporting local schools through nonprofit foundations
